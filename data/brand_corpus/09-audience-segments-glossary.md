# Audience Segments Glossary

*Cymbal Voyages Brand Studio · Agency onboarding packet · Last updated September 2026*

Briefs name audiences by segment key. The keys below are the only ones in use, and they match the campaign history and the ad performance records. A brief that needs a different cut of the audience describes it as a filter on one of these keys (for example, `lapsed_compass` limited to cold-weather and mild markets) rather than inventing a new key. Segments can overlap; a customer can be in `urban_explorers` and `active_compass` at the same time.

## Segments

| Key | Plain-English name | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| `all_customers` | Everyone in the CRM | Everyone in the CRM, members and non-members. About 50,000 contacts as of March 2026. |
| `prospects` | Prospects | Anonymous and first-time visitors with no account. Reached through paid social prospecting, paid search and affiliate partners. |
| `lapsed_compass_cold` | Lapsed members, cold markets | Cymbal Compass members in cold-weather markets with no booking in 12 or more months. The audience of the always-on Warm Escapes Retargeting program (CMP-002). |
| `lapsed_compass` | Lapsed members | Cymbal Compass members, any market, with no booking in 12 or more months. About 10,000 in cold and mild markets in August 2025. |
| `active_compass` | Active members | Cymbal Compass members who booked in the last 12 months. |
| `ski_enthusiasts` | Ski enthusiasts | Customers who viewed or booked ski destinations. |
| `adventure_seekers` | Adventure seekers | Customers who viewed or booked adventure destinations. |
| `cruise_interest` | Cruise interest | Customers who viewed or booked cruise itineraries. |
| `urban_explorers` | Urban explorers | Customers who viewed or booked city breaks. About 26,000 across all markets in July 2026. |

The behavioral segments (`ski_enthusiasts`, `adventure_seekers`, `cruise_interest`, `urban_explorers`) are built on viewing and booking activity in the last 18 months, and a customer drops out when that activity ages past the window. The Compass segments are built on enrollment and booking recency and update nightly.

## Related terms

**Cold-weather markets.** Chicago, Boston, Minneapolis, Detroit, Denver, Toronto, Cleveland and Milwaukee. These eight metros supply most warm-escape demand. Warm markets are Miami, Phoenix, Houston, Los Angeles and San Diego, where the warm-escape proposition does not apply. Every other market is mild. A brief that says "cold and mild markets" means everything except the five warm markets.

**Warm escapes.** Our signature category: winter and early-spring getaways to sun destinations in the Caribbean, Mexico, Hawaii, Central America and the Florida Keys. Customers in cold-weather markets start booking from late summer; the booking season runs August through January and travel runs December through April. The other categories are city breaks, ski, adventure and cruises.

**Booking.** A confirmed reservation. Cancelled reservations do not count toward campaign results, Compass tier, points, or the active and lapsed definitions. Booking targets and results in briefs are counted this way.

**Retargeting.** Paid media served to a person who has already shown intent on our site or app, such as viewing a warm destination or a package without booking. Our Warm Escapes Retargeting program (CMP-002) is the always-on example: paid social to `lapsed_compass_cold` members who browsed a warm destination, historically the most efficient paid program we run because it reaches people who have already told us what they want. Retargeting audiences carry a frequency cap of three impressions per person per seven days.

**Propensity score.** A model score from 0 to 1 of how likely a customer is to book a warm escape in the next 60 days, refreshed nightly from browsing, booking history, market and season. Briefs summarize an audience with the median score and the share at or above 0.30, the offer threshold in the decisioning policy; scores are right-skewed, so a median near 0.12 with a tenth of the audience at or above 0.30 is a strong audience. The score measures warm-escape intent only; for a city-break, ski, adventure or cruise audience it is context, not a targeting guide, and the brief should say so.

**Lapsed and active.** A member is active with a confirmed booking in the last 12 months, and lapsed with at least one booking ever and none in the last 12 months. The full definitions and the rules for talking to lapsed members are in the Cymbal Compass program summary.
