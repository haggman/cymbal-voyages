# Campaign Brief Template

*Cymbal Voyages Brand Studio · Agency onboarding packet · Last updated September 2026*

Every campaign brief at Cymbal Voyages uses the nine section headings below, in this order, with these exact names as level-2 headings. Do not rename, merge, reorder or omit a section. If a section does not apply, keep the heading and write one sentence explaining why. The Brand Studio agent must produce briefs in this structure and marketers review them against it. After launch, a tenth section, `## Retrospective`, is appended to the same document with results, what worked, what did not, and what we would do differently.

Reference the campaign by its identifier (for example CMP-003) and name in the title, and give the flight dates immediately under the title.

## Objective

*One or two sentences on the business outcome the campaign exists to produce, in the form "Do X for Y by Z." Name the category (warm escapes, city breaks, ski, adventure, cruises) and the season. Avoid objectives that describe activity ("run a paid social campaign") rather than outcome ("win back lapsed members ahead of the warm-escape booking season").*

## Audience

*Name the target segment by its key from the segment taxonomy (for example `lapsed_compass`), the markets (cold-weather, warm, mild or all), the audience size in contacts or matched users, and a propensity summary: the median propensity score for the audience and the share at or above 0.30 (the offer threshold in the decisioning policy). State any exclusions (recent bookers, unsubscribed, active reservations).*

## Insight

*The single observation about the audience or the moment that makes this campaign worth running, drawn from past retrospectives, survey data or booking patterns. One paragraph. If the insight is borrowed from another campaign, cite it by identifier.*

## Channels

*List the channels from the fixed taxonomy only: paid search, paid social, organic, email, direct, affiliate. For each, state its role (reach, reminder, conversion) and the frequency cap that applies. Name the primary paid channel if there is more than one.*

## Offer

*Describe the offer framing (percent off, bonus points, free night, urgency, or no offer), the exact mechanics, the deadline, and the conditions. Check the creative production standards for which framings are allowed for this audience; sitewide percent-off is not allowed for Compass members.*

## Budget and modeled reach

*Total budget in dollars, broken down by channel and by media versus production, with the daily paid spend and the number of flight days. State the source of the money (new budget, or reallocation from a named program). Modeled reach: unique people reached per channel and expected impressions. Points liability is carried by the Compass reserve and is not campaign cost.*

## KPIs

*The booking target (confirmed reservations; cancellations do not count), the ROI target using our formula (attributed revenue × 22% contribution margin − campaign cost) ÷ campaign cost, and two or three secondary measures such as cost per booking, email click rate or reactivation rate. State the attribution window.*

## Creative direction

*Imagery style from the approved set (beach couple, family pool, resort aerial, city skyline, adventure), the call to action from the approved set (Book now; See deals; Plan your escape; Claim offer), the headline territory, and the tone note for this audience. Name the test plan if creative variants will be tested.*

## Legal review notes

*Which disclaimers apply (price, loyalty points, both), any banned-phrase risks in the proposed copy, imagery rights, and the name of the reviewer and the date of sign-off. Leave the sign-off line blank until legal has reviewed.*

## Retrospective

*Added after the campaign closes, or at a stated checkpoint for campaigns still in flight. Results against every KPI with the actual numbers, what worked, what did not, what we would do differently, and any open questions for the next planning review. Numbers must match the campaign history record.*
