The mkt016 spike (item S2, run Sep 18 in a Qwiklabs project) found a problem with the data foundation you delivered, and I need a small fix, a regeneration and a re-stage.

## What happened

In BigQuery Studio I built a data agent over nine tables: `ad_performance`, `bookings`, `campaign_history`, `customer_month_status`, `customers`, `destinations`, `packages`, `plan` and `web_sessions`. I gave it **no instructions, no glossary and no verified queries**, then asked "Why did warm escapes bookings miss plan in August?" Its first answer was the entire story:

> In August 2026, warm escapes bookings missed plan by 701 bookings (−18.1%), driven almost entirely by cold-weather markets (−663 bookings or −33.5% vs. plan), because the marketing budget for the Warm Escapes Retargeting campaign (`CMP-002`, targeted specifically at `lapsed_compass_cold`) was reallocated on July 24, 2026, to fund the launch of Fall City Breaks 2026 (`CMP-012`).

The numbers are right, which is good news. But the lab depends on the cause being found by investigation. Task 1 teaches that the business definitions matter, and Task 2 has the marketer follow the thread from markets to cohort to conversion to spend. An agent that names CMP-002 and July 24 on its first try, with no help, undoes both.

When I added the instructions, glossary and a verified query, the agent broke the numbers down by market and cohort and did not jump to the campaign. That is the behavior we want. So the fix is to remove the giveaways from the data, not to change the instructions.

## The two leaks

**1. The `ad_performance` table description** (applied by `load.sh` from `schemas/_tables.json`). Delete this sentence:

Total paid spend is steady across the summer of 2026, but its allocation between campaigns changed on Jul 24, 2026.

so that the description reads only:

Daily paid-media results by campaign and market, Jul 1, 2025 – Sep 15, 2026. Only paid channels (paid_search, paid_social, affiliate) appear here; email and organic have no media spend.

It lives in `data/cvgen/schemas.py` (the source), `data/schemas/_tables.json` (generated) and `data/docs/data-dictionary.md` (generated by `sql/build_dictionary.py`). Change the source and regenerate the other two. Don't hand-edit them.

**2. The CMP-002 retrospective in `campaign_history`.** In `data/content/campaigns.json`, the CMP-002 `retrospective` ends with:

Budget was rotated to Fall City Breaks in late July 2026 to fund the launch.

Delete that sentence. The rest of the retrospective ("Historically our most efficient always-on program…") is a fair clue and should stay. The rotation must be discoverable only from the daily spend in `ad_performance`, where the walkthrough's spend query finds it.

## Also, please

- **Audit every other table and column description, and every free-text column in `campaign_history`,** for anything else that states the cause or the date outright: the rotation, the Jul 24 date tied to spend or budget, a conversion drop, or the lapsed/cold cohort described as underperforming.
