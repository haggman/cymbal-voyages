# Cymbal Compass Program Summary

*Cymbal Voyages Brand Studio · Agency onboarding packet · Last updated September 2026*

Cymbal Compass is our loyalty program. It is free to join, it has three earned tiers, and it is the single strongest lever we have for bringing customers back. This page states the facts every brief must agree with and the rules for talking to members.

## Tiers

| Tier | How you get there | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| None | Not enrolled | A customer with no Compass account. Earns no points. |
| Blue | Enrolled | Every member starts here. Earns points on every booking. |
| Silver | 4 bookings, or $8,000 lifetime spend | Priority support line and early access to warm-escape inventory each August. |
| Gold | 8 bookings, or $20,000 lifetime spend | Silver benefits plus a dedicated booking concierge and an annual bonus of 5,000 points. |

Tier is measured on confirmed bookings and lifetime spend since enrollment. A booking is a confirmed reservation; cancelled reservations do not count toward tier, points or any campaign result.

## Earning and redeeming

Members earn 10 points per dollar spent on any booking. 10,000 points is worth $100 toward a booking, and points can be applied at checkout in 1,000-point increments. Points expire after 24 months with no account activity; any booking resets the clock. Bonus points from a campaign post within 14 days of completed travel and carry the same terms. The points liability sits in the Compass reserve and is not counted as campaign cost; its value is reflected in contribution margin.

The Compass Anniversary runs every June. In 2026 (CMP-009) an email-only double-points offer to active members produced 1,340 bookings at an ROI of 8.5 on a $25,000 budget, the most efficient campaign in our history. It confirms the pattern: points move members more than price does.

## Lapsed and active

A member is **active** when they have a confirmed booking in the last 12 months. A member is **lapsed** when they have at least one booking ever and no booking in the last 12 months. Enrolled members who have never booked are neither; they are treated as prospects with an account. The segment keys are `active_compass`, `lapsed_compass` and, for the cold-weather subset that our warm-escape programs target, `lapsed_compass_cold`.

## What wins lapsed members back

Three things, per our history. Bonus points on a specific booking: CMP-003 Winter Sun Early Bird 2025 offered 5,000 bonus points on a warm escape booked by October 31 and brought back 1,960 lapsed members at an ROI of 4.3, 31 percent over target. A warm "plan your escape" invitation with beach-and-couple imagery: in the same campaign that combination converted at 14.9 percent of its test cell, ahead of every other variant. And a sense of being remembered: the "Welcome back" opener, the member's own points balance in the email, and a tone that assumes they will be back.

Two things do not work, and we have paid to learn it. Sitewide percent-off sales and urgency countdowns: in CMP-006 Spring Flash Sale 2026, Compass members converted at 0.7 percent against 1.3 percent for non-members, Silver and Gold members converted worst of all, and the survey told us the sale cheapened the brand for the customers who value it most. In the Winter Sun test, the percent-off cell with a 72-hour reminder converted worst and produced the most unsubscribes. Members read a discount as a coupon. They read points as recognition.

## How to talk to lapsed members

Open with "Welcome back" or a line that shows we noticed the gap without making the member feel watched ("It has been a while since Aruba"). Show their points balance and, if it applies, the date their points would expire, as a helpful fact rather than a threat. Make one specific offer, framed as bonus points on a named category with a real deadline stated once. Use beach-couple imagery for warm escapes and Plan your escape as the call to action. Do not use percent-off, countdowns, "last chance" or "we miss you" with an exclamation point (we do not use exclamation points anywhere). Every asset carries the loyalty points disclaimer from the legal claims list. If a lapsed member does not respond to two invitations in a season, rest them until the next season rather than escalating the offer.
